how to use this app

  1. Drag the scene to orbit the hero a full 360°.
  2. Tap demo to play Faded by Alan Walker.
  3. Tap microphone to react to music in your room.
  4. Pick a station; trains pass on their own.
  5. Open settings for turntable spin.

Tip: enable Turntable in settings for hands-free 360°.
